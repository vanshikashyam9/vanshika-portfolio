01 — where this started

It kept
forgetting.

I started my Computer Science degree in 2023, the same year these tools landed in front of me. Before that I’d never had anything like them. They were genuinely useful — for studying, for picking up skills faster than a textbook would let me, for getting unstuck at 1 a.m.

But something happened in the long conversations. Past a certain point the answers went strange. It would contradict what it had told me an hour earlier, lose the thread of what we were building, get confidently wrong about things it had been right about at the top of the chat.

Most people close the tab and open a fresh one. I wanted to know what had actually changed between the good answers and the bad ones.

Attention is a probability distribution. A distribution over more things is a thinner one.

Every token competes for a fixed budget of attention — softmax normalises the row to sum to one. Add more context and every earlier token’s share shrinks. The instruction you gave at the start isn’t deleted. It’s diluted.

That’s the simplified version, and I know it. Real degradation is also positional encodings extrapolating past their trained range, quantisation error compounding in a long KV cache, and instruction drift. But the softmax intuition was the door in, and I’ve been reading through it ever since.

It settled something I’d been carrying since I picked my degree: I didn’t want to only use these things. I wanted to be able to explain them.

αᵢ = exp(sᵢ) / Σⱼ exp(sⱼ). Hold the scores equal and every token’s share is exactly 1/n. Real attention is sharper than uniform — that is the point of it — but the ceiling falls the same way, and nothing you said early on is competing on equal footing by token two thousand.

RentScout AI

Full-stack · data · 2025

Before you send that deposit, check the listing.

Vancouver’s rental market runs on scams that target people who don’t know what rent should cost, don’t know their tenant rights, and need housing urgently. I was one of those people. Every rental site shows you listings; none help you judge one.

PythonFastAPIPostgreSQL SQLAlchemyNext.js 16TypeScript TailwindDockerGitHub Actions

The decision I’m proudest of

There is no legal way to bulk-scrape Craigslist or Marketplace, so the first version ranked generated sample listings. It looked richer in a demo and helped no one find housing — and the scam check was quietly pricing listings against that same invented data. I deleted the search page and rebuilt the price check on real CMHC survey rents. A tool that helps people avoid being deceived cannot cut corners on what it claims about itself.

Scam screen
Weighted signals across payment red flags, manufactured urgency, and prices benchmarked against CMHC average rents for that zone and bedroom count — not a flat threshold.
Market data
Real CMHC Rental Market Survey vacancy rates, 29 Metro Vancouver zones, 2022–2025, each with a plain-English read.
Budget planner
Live verdict from income and expenses, with a warning when rent crosses the 30%-of-income guideline.
Engineering
Dockerised front and back, JWT + bcrypt auth, deterministic seed data, CI running lint, typecheck, build and API smoke tests.
In progress
A retrieval-augmented assistant over the BC Residential Tenancy Act, answering with citations to the actual legislation.
